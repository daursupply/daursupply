### Who We Are

**DAUR** comes from the Urdu word **دور**, meaning an era. *Aik naya daur.*

We have worked in Pakistan's shoe industry for 23 years. In our workshops across Lahore, Sialkot, and Kasur, skilled artisans make, stitch, and cement fine men's leather footwear. For most of those years, we sold wholesale to well known brands that added their own label and a heavy brand margin on top.

DAUR Supply is our direct answer. Every pair of leather sneakers, formal shoes, chelsea boots, and casual slip ons goes straight from the bench to your door. Each pair is inspected by hand and finished with thick matte black cotton laces, a laser engraved metal dubrae, and the DAUR seal.

### How We Work & Customer Policies

* **Direct from the Bench & Dispatched from Islamabad:** Orders are confirmed via call or WhatsApp and dispatched within 24 hours on working days. Most cities receive their packages in 2 to 4 days.
* **Care Deposits & Flexible Payment:** A small Care deposit by bank transfer reserves your size and puts your name on the box (Rs. 500 for orders up to Rs. 3,000; Rs. 1,000 up to Rs. 8,000; Rs. 1,500 up to Rs. 15,000; full payment in advance for orders above Rs. 15,000). The remaining balance is paid on delivery. 
* **Automatic Bag Rewards:** No codes needed. Discounts apply automatically based on your bag total (e.g., Rs. 200 off from Rs. 5,500, Rs. 400 off from Rs. 9,000, and an extra Rs. 100 off when paid in full).
* **Guarantees & Exchanges:** Full price pairs are backed by a 5 day size exchange (unworn, boxed, and sealed). Stitching or sole failures in normal wear are covered for 30 days.

### A Share That Is Not Ours to Keep (Our Giving)

At least 5 percent of our profit, never added to your price, is pooled from every pair and given at the end of each quarter to verified cases of need that our team checks personally. Every quarterly giving amount and date is published publicly.
